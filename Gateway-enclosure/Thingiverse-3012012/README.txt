Enclosure for LoraWan TTN Gateway: Raspberry Pi + RAK831 by Amedee on Thingiverse: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3012012

Summary:
Enclosure for RAK831 LoraWan Gateway.The enclosure allows air circulation to avoid overheating.BOMAside the Raspberry Pi, the RAK831 LoraWan gateway and its converter board you will need:4 M2.5x12 to secure the Raspberry Pi4 M3x8 + 3 M3 nuts for the feet4 M3x8 round head to close the enclosure

Addition by C. Pham from University of Pau, France:
On May 27th, 2026, with the help of Cédric Arrabie from the UPPA's LATEP laboratory, a new back panel with the hole for the antenna connector of our [LoRa RFM95W/RFM96W breakout board](https://github.com/CongducPham/PEPR_AgriFutur/blob/main/PCBs/RFM95Breakout_2020-11-13-long.zip) has been designed to replace the initial design for the now obsolete RAK831 concentrator hat. The new file is `TTNRak831PanelBackNew.stl`. In addition, a plastic cover for the OLED screen has also been designed, see `TTNRak831OledCover.stl`.
